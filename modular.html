Modular installation and fit-out

We install and fit out modular buildings on site and in the factory. Our team has delivered packages for the MOD, the RAF, healthcare, education, retail and commercial clients.

Services

What we install.

Full site installation

Complete installation of the building on site, including external cladding, parapets and finishing works.

Internal joinery

Doors, skirting and cubicles, fitted on site or in the factory.

Dry lining and insulation

Dry lining for each wall type, with insulation to the project specification, on site or in the factory.
