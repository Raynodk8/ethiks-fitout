Privacy policy.

Who we are

Ethiks is the trading name of Ethiks Site Services Ltd, company number 10755925, registered office 167-169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London, England, W1W 5PF. Ethiks Site Services Ltd is the controller of the personal information described here.

If you ask us for a quote

We collect your name, your company if you have one, your phone number and email address, and what you tell us about the job. We use this to reply to you, to price the work and, if you go ahead, to carry it out and invoice it.

If you register to work with us

We collect your name, contact details, trade and CSCS card number. We use this to contact you about work and to check that you hold the cards a site requires.

Our lawful reasons

We rely on our legitimate interests in running a contracting business, on the steps needed to enter into and carry out a contract with you, and on our legal duties, including health and safety and tax record keeping.

Who we share it with

We share information only where the work needs it: with the client or main contractor on a project you are working on, and with our accountants and insurers. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we are dealing with them and for a reasonable time afterwards. Contract, tax and health and safety records are kept for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we use it. Email privacy@ethiks.co.uk or write to us at the registered office above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
