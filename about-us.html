About us

It's not what we do, it's why we do it

Ethiks is a private company, owned by the people who manage it and built on loyalty and service.

Who we are

We are a specialist fit-out contractor. Our management team and workforce are hands-on: we take the time to understand what each client needs, then deliver it.

Quality, service and value carry equal weight with us. We invest in developing our people, because the standard of the work depends on them.

  • An ethical culture, built on loyalty and service
  • Managers who deal with you directly
  • An experienced management team and workforce
  • Experience across retail, commercial and residential work

How we manage a project

From tender through to handover, our service is built around what the client needs.

  • We listen first. We work out exactly what you need before we price it.
  • We protect the programme. Dates matter on every project. We track progress and deal with problems early.
  • We protect the design. What was drawn is what gets built.
  • We keep you informed. Open communication at every stage, with no surprises at handover.

Outside the UK, our project management consulting branch works from the UAE. Project management in the UAE.
